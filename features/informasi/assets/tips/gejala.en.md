The most common symptoms are fever, cough, tiredness, and loss of taste or smell. Others include sore throat, headache, and aches and pains. A serious sign is difficulty breathing or shortness of breath. Some people are infected without any symptoms.

If you have symptoms or tested positive, contact your nearest community health centre or health facility and follow the advice of health workers.

## Danger signs: go to a health facility now

- Shortness of breath or difficulty breathing.
- Chest pain.
- Confusion, or being unable to speak or move as usual.
- More than 20 breaths per minute. In mild illness, breathing is 12 to 20 breaths per minute.
- Blood oxygen saturation below 95%, if you have an oximeter. In mild illness, saturation is above 95%.
- Symptoms getting worse.

Contact your community health centre, the nearest health facility or your monitoring health worker right away if you see these signs.

Call 119 or go to the emergency department of a hospital now if you have severe shortness of breath, chest pain, confusion, more than 30 breaths per minute, or oxygen saturation below 93%. The Ministry of Health classes more than 30 breaths per minute or saturation below 93% as severe illness.

For a medical emergency, call 119, the Ministry of Health medical emergency service.

This guide is for adults. For children, pregnant women, or if you have another illness, ask a health worker.
