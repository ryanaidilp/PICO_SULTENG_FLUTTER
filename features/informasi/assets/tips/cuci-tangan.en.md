People can be infected if they touch their eyes, nose or mouth after touching a contaminated surface.

## What you can do

- Wash your hands with soap and water, or with an alcohol-based hand rub.
- Cover your nose and mouth when you cough or sneeze.

Hand dryers are not effective at killing the coronavirus. Wash your hands with soap and water, or use an alcohol-based hand rub.
