## First steps

If you have symptoms or tested positive, get advice right away through telemedicine or your local community health centre. Health workers do the monitoring.

You can isolate at home or in a central isolation facility. Hospitals are kept first for patients with moderate, severe or critical illness, and those who need oxygen.

## What to prepare

1. Have a thermometer and an oximeter.
2. Keep the number of your monitoring health worker or community health centre, to report if you get worse.
3. Use a separate bedroom if you can.
4. Use your own dishes and bathing items.
5. If you have another illness, keep taking your regular medicine as your doctor advises.

## Daily care

- Measure and write down your temperature and oxygen saturation twice a day, morning and evening.
- Tell your monitoring health worker or community health centre if your temperature goes above 38 °C.
- Wear a mask when you leave your room and when you meet family. Keep your distance and cover your mouth when you cough or sneeze.
- Wash your hands often with soap and running water, or with hand sanitiser.
- Wash dishes and cups right away with water and soap.
- Open the bedroom window now and then and clean the room every day.

The danger signs are in the article [Symptoms and when to go to a health facility](/info/tips/gejala).
