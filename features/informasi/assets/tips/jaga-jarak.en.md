The virus leaves the mouth or nose of an infected person in small liquid particles. Other people can breathe them in. It spreads mainly between people who are near each other, and the risk is higher when they are close for a long time.

People without symptoms can also pass the virus on, although how often this happens is not clear.

## What you can do

- Keep at least one metre away from others, even if they look well.
